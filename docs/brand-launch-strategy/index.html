Reputation Building & Market Entry

Brand Launch Strategy Built Around Reputation From Day One

Reputation Building & Market Entry is a reputation building strategy that constructs a positive information field from scratch: search presence, mention monitoring and business media placement for a new country, a new brand or a rebrand where almost nothing exists online yet. We build the profile a new audience finds — as one program run by a dedicated market-entry team — instead of leaving the first impression to whatever surfaces first.
NDA from the first contact

What Reputation Building & Market Entry covers

  • Search & SERP presence — Google, Bing and knowledge panels built from zero for the new name or market
  • Mention & Narrative baseline — social listening live from day one, so the first signals get caught as the footprint forms
  • Business & Industry Media Placement — the market's first real signal about the company, not a single press release
  • Growth Logic, not Insurance — priced against the value of the market entered, not against a risk being hedged
  • Dedicated Build Team, Not a Self-serve Dashboard — strategist and content team executing the build, plus platform access

What problems we help to resolve

Signs Your Brand Needs to Build Its Reputation From Zero

Entering Blind

You're opening in a new country or vertical, and a search for the company name returns nothing — or worse, a namesake with no connection to you at all: no online presence, no reviews, no word-of-mouth, and nothing showing how the brand is perceived either way.

No Narrative to Inherit

There's no existing coverage, review or AI answer to correct, because there's nothing there yet — no brand perception to track yet, positive or negative — and a blank information field is its own kind of exposure.

Rebrand With no Digital Record

A rename, spin-off or relaunch leaves years of equity behind — building a strong brand reputation means the new name starts from zero in search, reviews and AI answers alike, on launch day, carrying none of the trust, loyalty or core values customers associated with the old name.

Growth Outrunning the Profile

Expansion, funding or a new market launch is moving faster than the digital footprint — investors, partners and hires find less online than the business actually is — the same gap potential customers hit the moment they start researching you.

What it Takes to Build a Brand Reputation Strategy From Zero

A launch press release and an ad campaign both fade inside a news cycle. What a new market actually indexes and what an AI model reads back when someone asks about the new name — is whatever search results, mentions and media coverage and online credibility exist six months later. Building search presence without setting up mention monitoring, or placing media coverage with nobody tracking how it lands, leaves exactly the gap that shows up in the first due-diligence check or the first analyst query.
We build the information field a new market will find — search presence, mention baseline and media placement — as one coordinated program, not a press release and a hope.

Market pattern

Starbucks Korea

Consumer · Culturally-insensitive campaign, uncontained
What happened
A “Tank Day” promotion referencing military imagery landed on the anniversary of the 1980 Gwangju massacre. By week 3 the CEO was dismissed; by week 4 the parent company's chairman issued a public apology; by week 5 government ministries began cancelling contracts; by week 6 every store closed nationwide for mandatory history training, with no timeline for a sales recovery.
Mechanic
Each week without a coordinated response escalated the story to a new audience — customers, then the corporate parent, then government stakeholders, then employees. What could have been contained in the first 72 hours instead spent six weeks compounding into a systemic crisis with no single fix.
The Guardian, 16 Jun 2026

What we don't promise

The Limits of This Solution

Not a Press Release

We're not selling one press push around launch day. If all a market entry needs is announcement coverage, a launch-focused PR agency for that single moment is the cheaper option.

No Guaranteed Coverage

We can't guarantee a specific media placement, ranking or sentiment number: only compliant methods, and we say so upfront — no shortcuts that fake authenticity or manufacture online reviews.

Not a Fix for an Existing Footprint

If the brand already has search results, reviews or an AI answer that needs correcting, that's Search Engine Reputation Management or Brand Reputation & Narrative Management.

We Don't Replace Your Team

We build alongside the in-house marketing, comms or country-launch team — as build capacity, not a replacement for the function.

What You're Actually Paying For

You're not Pricing Content

You're pricing what entering a market invisibly actually costs — a partner who can't find the company, a candidate who finds nothing and never applies, a due-diligence check that comes back thin. This is priced against that gap, not against a headcount of writers — it's an investment in the brand's reputation and the brand's trustworthiness that a market judges before you ever say a word — this is what it costs to attract new customers who expect to find you before they ever pick up the phone.

We Don't Price Like a Launch Agency

This runs as ongoing infrastructure sized to the market being entered — not a flat campaign fee that ends the day the press cycle does — the same continuity a leader in your industry uses to connect with your audience well after launch day.

The Price Includes the Search & Listening Modules
