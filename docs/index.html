Digital Risk Protection (DRP) is the practice of identifying, monitoring and neutralising threats to a company's digital profile before they cause financial or operational damage. Unlike traditional ORM, DRP is proactive, detecting signals early across all 5 elements: AI representation, search results, brand mentions, reviews and narrative tone.

Traditional ORM agencies react — they clean up damage after it's done.
