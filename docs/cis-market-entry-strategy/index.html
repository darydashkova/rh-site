Regional specifics

What's Actually Different About The CIS Market Landscape
A go-to-market build ported from a Western template misses the infrastructure this audience actually uses. Four layers of the information field change, not just the language.
  • Search: Yandex, not Just Google
    Yandex holds the larger market share of search across the CIS — a build tuned only for Google leaves the region's default engine untouched.
  • Social: VK and Telegram, Not Meta
    The conversation runs on VK and Telegram channels — monitoring scoped to Instagram or X misses where this market reacts first.
  • Reviews: 2GIS, Otzovik, hh.ru
    Consumer trust runs through 2GIS and Otzovik; employer reputation through hh.ru — a Trustpilot-and-Glassdoor build leaves these platforms empty.
  • Media: RBC, Kommersant, Vedomosti
    Business credibility is read through RBC, Kommersant, Vedomosti and sector titles — a translated release rarely lands where due-diligence teams check.

Case Studies

What Go-to-market Risk Actually Looks Like For CIS Market Players
None of the four cases below are Reputation House clients — all four are documented, sourced CIS market events. Each "What Happened" box is the public record. Each "If They Had Worked With RH" box is our own analytical read of where a monitored, planned information field would have changed the outcome — and where it honestly wouldn't have.
Wildberries
E-commerce marketplace · shareholder conflict, CIS
What happened:
A shareholder conflict went public — raiding accusations involving roughly 37 billion rubles and a shooting at the Moscow office with casualties. The brand became associated with violence and corporate chaos; vendor and investor trust came into question. Viral negative coverage held for months and drove the platform's first-ever seller outflow, at 24.5%.

If Wildberries had worked with RH:
Ongoing monitoring catches a conflict entering its acute phase before each new development becomes its own media event. A managed counter-narrative doesn't end a shareholder war, but it keeps public chaos from becoming the platform's defining market image. Result: a 24.5% seller outflow is the cost of a narrative vacuum the media filled, not the company. Resolving the dispute isn't something RH can do. Occupying the narrative vacuum ahead of the market is.

Profil magazine, 2024–2026; Kommersant/Vostock Photo coverage. Reputation House analysis, not a client engagement.
Rendez-Vous
Retail chain · internal/external narrative gap, CIS
What happened:
The retailer spent roughly 30 million rubles on an anniversary press tour featuring Patricia Kaas, helicopters and Courchevel dinners. It then emerged that Moscow store staff had salaries and bonuses cut in 2025. Against a wartime backdrop, the contrast read publicly as "feasting while staff go without" and dominated the agenda for a month.

If Rendez-Vous had worked with RH:
A pre-launch information-field analysis reads the planned narrative against the internal reality before the tour ships, not after — flagging that a luxury event staged against reduced staff pay will be read this way before it airs. Result: 30 million rubles doesn't convert into a month of viral negative coverage — the audit costs a fraction of the campaign it would have stopped. Setting salaries is the company's call, not RH's. Seeing the gap between the storefront and the backstage before the market does is.

CIS retail and business press coverage, January 2026. Reputation House analysis, not a client engagement.
Yandex
Technology · corporate reorganization, CIS
What happened:
A business split, the exit of foreign managers and the founder's distancing from the company left a narrative vacuum. The frame "Yandex stopped being a technology company" formed and spread with no counter-narrative from the brand itself.

If Yandex had worked with RH:
A managed transition narrative — what's happening, why, where the company is headed — gets prepared before a strategic event like a split or an ownership change, and published as it unfolds, so the field isn't left empty for the market to fill. Result: investment perception and deal reception are protected at the moment they're worth the most. The reorganization structure is the company's decision, not RH's. Making the company the author of its own story in public is what RH does.

Vedomosti Kapital, 2026; T-Bank Media, 2026. Reputation House analysis, not a client engagement.
