What we don't promise

The Limits of This Solution

We don't touch your search results

Continuous Monitoring watches and flags. Actively changing what ranks — search, reviews, narrative — is RH Control, a separate program.

We don't fight an active crisis

If a threat is unfolding right now, that's RH Defence — not this program. See the crosslink below.

No 100% guarantee

The public information field depends on algorithms and platforms we don't control. We commit to systematic coverage and honest reporting — not a promise to catch literally everything.

Not a replacement for your team

We escalate when risk is confirmed. Your in-house risk, legal or comms team still owns the decision — we don't run it instead of them.
