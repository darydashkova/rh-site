The Problem

The market
has changed

Digital reputation is no longer a PR concern. A company's digital presence is an asset, exposed to coordinated information attacks, misleading AI answers and toxic search results. Enterprises need continuous monitoring that surfaces a threat before it compounds.

01

Infofield is a business risk

Every brand mention across search engines, AI systems, review sites, and digital channels directly impacts revenue, market access, and customer trust.

02

Info attacks ×2 per year

Coordinated reputational attacks are doubling annually — most companies detect them far too late to mitigate the damage.

03

Data exists — control doesn't

Companies collect monitoring data but lack the infrastructure to act before signals escalate into crises.

04

Crises outpace management

By the time internal teams notice a threat, the narrative has already spread across multiple channels.
