How Payment Works

Three Ways You Can Pay for Reputation Management

Reputation management services pricing isn't a single price list — there are three payment models, and which one applies depends on whether the work is ongoing, finite, or urgent. Most reputation management fees fall into one of these three billing shapes, whatever provider you use, so it helps to know which one you're actually buying.

Most common

Retainer

Billed monthly, scoped to an agreed KPI cycle — reviewed and reset every quarter. The default model for ongoing coverage.

Used for: RH Detection, RH Control, RH Personal.

Finite scope

Project

A fixed deliverable, fixed fee, fixed timeline — no recurring billing. Used where there's a clear finish line.

Used for: Risk Check diagnostics, negative content removal, pre-deal and pre-IPO profile preparation.

Active crisis

Project + Urgency Premium

Priced as a project because the engagement is time-boxed by the crisis itself, with a premium for 24/7 priority response.

Used for: RH Defence, acute RH Personal Crisis response.

⌁

Continuous Management

Most engagements land on a retainer because most reputational risk isn't a one-time problem — it's monitored, reported and adjusted every quarter. You're not buying a fixed task; you're buying a KPI commitment for a period, and scope and price follow from what it takes to hit it.

How The Price Is Set

From First Call to Fixed Reputation Management Pricing

The price isn't quoted cold — it's built with you, in four steps, before anything is signed.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We talk through the situation and the request — what's at risk, who's checking, and on what timeline.

  2. 02

    Analytics

    We map the current information field: the same five-element view as Risk Check, done in full. Search, mentions, AI representation, reviews, narrative and tone.

  3. 03

    KPI & timeline proposal

    Based on the findings, we propose specific KPIs — coverage, SLA, share-of-voice targets — and the timeline to hit them.

  4. 04

    Scope & price locked

    Scope of work and price follow the agreed KPIs and timeline, confirmed in the proposal before work starts.

Faster

6 months

More work concentrated per month to hit the same KPIs sooner — higher monthly spend, shorter total commitment.

Standard

12 months

Same end state, paced over a longer runway — lower monthly spend, longer total commitment.

The destination is usually the same either way — the question step 03 answers is how much gets spent per month to get there.
