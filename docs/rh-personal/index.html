RH Personal is an online reputation management service for individuals — executives, founders and professionals who need to manage what the world finds about them online.

How Online Reputation Management Works

Companies' reality without real-time monitoring

Information Blindness in Action: What Your Name Returns Without Online Reputation Management

01

"The article is three years old. It still ranks first when anyone searches my name."

Your Search Results Are Telling a Story You Haven't Approved

Personal SERP is the first due diligence anyone runs. An old controversy, a misattributed quote, or a crisis-era article ranks permanently — and shapes decisions made by investors, partners, and boards before a single conversation happens. Most executives have never systematically reviewed what their own name returns in Google search results, let alone taken steps to control the narrative around it. Managing your online reputation proactively is the difference between shaping that first impression and discovering it too late.

02

"The acquisition fell through. Their DD team had found three articles."

AI Is Building Your Profile Without You

AI platforms construct a personal profile from indexed content — regardless of accuracy, recency, or context. Investors, executive search firms, and counterparties now ask AI about people before they make contact. That profile is being formed right now, and you are not in it. There's no scrutiny applied to what gets surfaced, no way to flag what's outdated or misleading. Your LinkedIn presence, your search engine results, your media coverage — all of it feeds what AI systems say when someone asks about you.

03

"There's almost nothing about me online. I thought that was safe."

Your Digital Footprint Has Gaps — and Gaps Are a Risk

For executives and founders, low digital visibility is not privacy — it is an information vacuum that others fill. In due diligence, KYC screening, and board onboarding, an absent or minimal digital footprint raises questions. A controlled, accurate online presence is an asset. Its absence is a liability. When counterparties search for you online and find nothing, they don't assume the best. ORM (online reputation management) for individuals starts with understanding what already exists — and what should.

04

"We have monitoring. We have no one to act on it."

The Company's Crisis Became Your Record

When an organization faces a reputational event, its executive leaders carry the digital footprint long after the corporate recovery. A failed venture, a leadership change, a public dispute — the company's narrative is managed. Yours rarely is. The consequences of negative content spillover onto individuals are well-documented: deal delays, board scrutiny, and prolonged remediation. Good reputation doesn't transfer automatically when a leader moves on.

05

"Someone wrote about me in a closed investor forum. I found out four months later."

No Visibility Into What Already Exists About You

Personal reputation monitoring is almost never systematic. Mentions in Telegram channels, niche professional communities, regional media, and investor networks surface too late — or not at all. By the time you know about a problem, opinions have already been formed. Google alerts catch a fraction of what's being said. Protecting your online reputation requires monitoring across search engines, closed networks, and AI platforms simultaneously.

06

"The fund ran a background check. Three things came up I didn't know existed."

KYC, Due Diligence, and Compliance Are Now Personal

Personal digital reputation is a formal transaction variable. KYC/AML screening, LP due diligence, board onboarding, and cross-border compliance reviews all include a personal online reputation component. What exists about you online can block or delay financial and professional decisions — without your knowledge. Negative search results and negative online content don't disappear on their own — they compound.

Your name is a business variable — is it managed?

What Happens After You Submit a Request

How It Works: From Diagnostics to Ongoing Reputation Management

Your personal reputation is being formed right now — in search algorithms, AI training data, and the content ecosystem around your name. The earlier it is managed, the less remediation is required. Proactive reputation management is always less expensive — in time, cost, and peace of mind — than a rapid-response crisis after the fact.

STEP 01

Personal Diagnostics

We audit your complete digital presence — everything that exists, ranked by visibility and risk. You see exactly where you stand before any operations begin. This includes what surfaces in a standard KYC process, what any Google search returns under your name, what search engine algorithms surface for your keywords, and how AI tools describe you. Understanding what currently exists is the foundation of managing your personal reputation effectively.

STEP 02

Strategy

We define what your controlled digital identity should look like — what to build, what to correct, what to suppress, and what narrative to establish for the audiences that matter. We determine what positive online presence needs to exist, how to build trust with counterparties who will search your name alongside your company name, and whether you need a personal website as part of the infrastructure.

STEP 03

Platform & Operations Launch

Monitoring activated across all personal channels. Operations begin on your SERP, AI representation, content in search, and social media presence. We establish a positive online presence where it needs to exist — in search, in professional directories, in LinkedIn, in media.

STEP 04

Ongoing Management

Monthly reporting, continuous operations, proactive alerts — your personal digital presence stays aligned with where you are now, not where you were. We monitor for negative results emerging from closed networks, catch drift in how AI describes you, and manage your reputation effectively as circumstances change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Personal reputation management is the ongoing process of monitoring, protecting, and actively shaping what exists about an individual online — across search results, AI platforms, media, and professional networks. For executives, managing your online reputation includes both proactive personal brand building and reactive suppression or correction of negative content. It is not the same as corporate brand management, and it isn't something that gets managed by default just because your company has a PR team.

Anyone who is subject to due diligence, KYC screening, investor research, or public scrutiny. That typically means founders, CEOs, C-suite executives, UHNWI individuals, and anyone entering a high-visibility role or transaction. It also includes individuals rebuilding after a public incident, executives stepping into board roles, and professionals who want to monitor their online reputation systematically rather than discovering issues mid-transaction.

At minimum: a full audit of your personal digital presence, SERP management for your name, AI-platform monitoring and correction, and ongoing monitoring of new mentions across open and semi-closed networks. Full personal reputation management services add content creation, bylined placements, crisis response, and compliance documentation. ORM as a service for executives should address everything from negative search results to outdated AI profiles — and provide a documented record for compliance contexts.

Corporate reputation management is built around brand monitoring, media relations, and company-level narrative. A reputation management firm that works with brands is not automatically equipped for individual client work.
