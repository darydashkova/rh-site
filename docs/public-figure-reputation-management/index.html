Celebrity & Public Figure Reputation Management

We reconcile the scattered public record already built up around your name — search results, media coverage, AI-generated answers — into one coherent, defensible profile, so a journalist, partner or new audience finds a consistent story instead of conflicting fragments. This is profile integrity for someone already in the public eye — not personal PR built for attention.
NDA from the first contact

What Celebrity & Public Figure Reputation Management covers

  • Search-Record Alignment — the search engine results under your name reconciled into one consistent, accurate narrative
  • Media-Record Reconciliation — prior coverage and interviews cross-checked for contradictions before a journalist finds them
  • AI-Representation Correction — a consistent, accurate answer across ChatGPT, Gemini and other assistants when someone asks who you are
  • One Coordinated Narrative — search, media and AI representation managed as a single story, not three unrelated channels
  • Confidential, NDA-Bound Team — coordinated with whichever publicist or advisors are already on the file; the engagement is never made public

What problems we help to resolve

Signs You Need Online Reputation Management as a Public Figure

Four patterns come up in almost every audit we run for a public figure. If two or more of them sound familiar, the record under your name is already working against you.

A Search Result You Don't Recognize

You search your own name and the top results are outdated, mismatched or belong to someone else entirely — not because there’s too little about you, but because nobody ever reconciled the online image those results add up to.

An AI Answer That Gets You Wrong

Ask ChatGPT or Gemini who you are and the answer is outdated, incomplete, or hedges between two conflicting versions of your own biography.

Coverage That Contradicts Itself

Different interviews and profiles tell different versions of your career or background, because no one has ever coordinated the message across them.

Visibility Without a Narrative

You’re recognized, quoted and invited to speak — but there’s no coherent through-line connecting the coverage, so every new mention starts from scratch and the personal brand resets.

What we do

Celebrity Reputation Management Strategies: From a Scattered Record to One Story

Reconciling a scattered record runs in four stages, in this order: audit, plan, coordinated correction, then maintenance. Most of the hard thinking happens in the first two.
01

Confidential Reputation Audit by ORM Experts

Every piece of online content under your name is mapped — search results, past coverage, AI-generated answers, social media — and every place they disagree with each other or with the facts.
02

Reconciliation Plan for Your Online Presence

A sequenced plan to align search, media record and AI representation around one accurate narrative, coordinated with any publicist, social media management or comms team already on the file.
03

Alignment Begins Across Search Results and Media

Corrections and additions land in a coordinated sequence — not one channel first and the others months later.
04

Ongoing Maintenance and Monitoring

New coverage and mentions are checked proactively against the established narrative as they appear; most clients move into ongoing monitoring, which is what protects the reputation over time.

The investment logic

What You’re Actually Paying a Reputation Management Agency For

You're Not Pricing Buzz

You're pricing the cost of a scattered online reputation — the deal, invitation or interview that quietly goes to someone whose story is easier to verify at a glance. A coherent, defensible profile is priced against exactly that competitive cost, not against a headcount of publicists. A positive reputation that survives a background check is the actual deliverable.

We Don't Price Like a Talent Agency

This prices for whether your public record is accurate and consistent the first time anyone checks — a different deliverable, priced against a different risk than coverage volume or buzz. Maintaining a positive online presence is a byproduct of accuracy here, not the sales pitch.

The Price Includes Your Public Record, Not a Tool

Search-record alignment, media reconciliation and AI-representation correction, plus the team maintaining all three — a done-for-you profile, not a self-serve dashboard you'd have to operate yourself.

It's an Ongoing Retainer

A public figure's record keeps accumulating new coverage and mentions. Celebrity reputation management runs as a monthly retainer so the profile stays coherent as things change, not a one-time fix that drifts out of date. Public figures who manage their online reputation continuously are the ones whose record still holds up years later, which is the only long-term success worth pricing.
