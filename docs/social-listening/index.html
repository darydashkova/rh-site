What is Social Listening? Quick Definition

Social listening is tracking what people say about your brand across social platforms, forums, blogs, video and press, then reading it for tone and risk — not just counting how many times you were mentioned. Social monitoring is the narrower version of that: watching a channel for volume and alerts. Most brands actually want something in between: a social listening platform that also has someone reading it. Whatever you call the category: social listening software or social media monitoring software — that's the version this page describes.

The investment logic

What You’re Actually Paying For

You're Not Pricing Hours

You're pricing the cost of a missed mention. Hershey's sentiment shift went unwatched until it became a public boycott; McDonald's watched one CEO joke spiral into a second scandal within days. This is priced against that, not against a headcount of analysts.

We Don't Price Like a Listening Dashboard

A cheap social media listening service gets you a login and raw data. So do most social media monitoring services and most social media monitoring companies — or a social media monitoring agency — pitching a bigger seat count. This gets you an analyst reading that data every day and escalating what's real — interpretation and accountability are part of the price, not an upsell.

The Price Includes the Full Platform

Mention metrics, dynamics, top sources and authors, tagged narratives, and a sentiment engine that self-learns from your corrections — the kind of depth a basic social monitoring platform doesn't attempt on its own, plus an analyst reading it daily. It's social media monitoring and analysis built for risk first, not a marketing dashboard built for campaign reporting — though the same feed tells you plenty about both. It doesn't include search results, AI answers or reviews; those live under the full Detection retainer. The retainer sits in the same band as the full contour because the cost driver isn't which modules are switched on — it's an analyst triaging every mention, across every channel and language, every day. Watching one channel properly costs nearly as much as watching five.

It's a Retainer, not a Project

Mentions accumulate silently and spike without warning — a one-time scan freezes a single day; a retainer keeps watching every day after. That's why it's billed monthly, like any function that has to run continuously.
