Why audit is the first mandatory step

Fixing A Problem You Haven't Measured Costs The Most

Most companies discover their information field through the damage: a deal that goes quiet, a bank that asks an unexpected question, a hiring funnel that dries up. By then you are paying for both: the problem itself and the guesswork.

Acting Blind
  • You treat what you noticed, not what is costing you. The loudest complaint is rarely the most important one.
  • You buy services by name, reviews, PR, SEO or market research companies, with no way to know which one moves the number.
  • You have no baseline, so you can never prove the work paid for itself.
  • Blind spots stay blind: suggestions, AI answers, HR platforms, regional review sites, closed communities.
  • You cannot distinguish an organic complaint from a coordinated attack, and they need opposite responses.
Acting with the Audit Done
  • You know which surface is actually leaking money, and in which market.
  • Every proposed action is tied to a finding, and every finding to a number.
  • You get a baseline. Every month afterwards is measured against it.
  • You see the whole field at once, including the surfaces you didn't know were being read.
  • You can walk into a board, a bank or a diligence room with evidence instead of an impression.

What we analyse

Five Places Where Your Brand Online Reputation is Formed

Positive, negative or neutral online reputation — all of it shapes buyers, banks, regulators and candidates decisions on your brand. Each direction is scored separately, then rolled into one index. This index shows brand performance the way the market actually perceive it, across target audiences and regions, so leadership can make informed decisions, backed by something data-driven, instead of guessing.

Clearer Consumer Insight, Not Just Sentiment
We track what keeps repeating, not just the tone of mentions: the same complaint, the same phrase in a search suggestion, the same flag in a screening database. That is consumer's insights you can act on. It also helps uncover blind spots by region and audience segmentation, so you can identify opportunities as well as risks, and see with real clarity where you stand out from the competition around you. In a crowded market, that's what gives you an advantage.
